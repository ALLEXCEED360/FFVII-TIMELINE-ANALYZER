# 0029 — Credits, as a staff roll

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-07
- **Builds on:** [0015](0015-game-menu-design.md), which added the page, and [0020](0020-modern-look.md), whose look it takes.

## Context

The owner asked to move on to the credits page, the last page still in the earlier design: a Bodoni title, uppercase italic section titles, three long lists of small artwork rows, typefaces and the icon and cursor as plain text rows, and the project's licences as a bulleted list.

## Decision

- **Credits**, under _Thanks to_, with the rights notice in plain words: Final Fantasy VII and all its names, characters and artwork belong to Square Enix; this is a non-commercial fan project, not made, approved or endorsed by Square Enix.
- **A staff roll**, centred, as at the end of the game: each role in small capitals with the names under it — the four games, _Square Enix_; _Artwork_, every artist named in the artwork manifest; _Site icon_; _Pointer_; _Lettering_, every typeface's designer; and _Every fact checked against_ the games themselves, with a link to how. The names come from the manifest and the list of typefaces, so the roll can't fall out of step with them.
- **The pictures:** what they are and where they came from in a paragraph, how they were prepared as four short notes, and a choice of _Everything_, _Key art_, _Characters_ or _Places_ over a grid of cards — each picture whole where it's a figure or a sketch, its title, artist and _See the original ↗_.
- **The lettering:** a card per typeface, its sample set in its own letters, who made it, where the site uses it, and its licence.
- **This project:** the code, the facts and writing, and the research, each a card saying in a sentence what may be done with it.

## Consequences

- ✅ The page reads like the end of the game, and each credit says what it's for.
- ❌ None of note.

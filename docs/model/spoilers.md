# Spoilers

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-27

## Policy: lenient

The original came out in 1997. The app compares whole stories — endings included — so hiding content would defeat its purpose.

- **No spoiler gate.** There is no "how far have you played" setting, and nothing in the data carries a revealed-in marker.
- **One notice.** On the first visit, a short dismissible notice says the app covers the full stories of every included title. Dismissal is remembered per browser.
- **IDs, URLs and names** use the best-known names, with no spoiler-safe naming (`conventions/ids.md` §5).
- **Order of reveal still matters** as information, not as protection: the play-order timeline (`model/chronology.md` §8) and `false_account` depictions show _when_ a title reveals something, which is part of comparing titles.

## Revisit when

- **_Revelation_ is released (2027).** The newest title may justify a single opt-in "hide the newest title's ending" toggle for a few months. Decide then; nothing in the model needs to change now.
